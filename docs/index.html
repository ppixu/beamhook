The problem

One play/pause key. Too many apps fighting over it.

Open a YouTube video and macOS quietly hands your media keys to Safari. Apple Music grabs them the moment it launches. You hit play expecting Spotify and pause a video three tabs away — and the only reliable fix is to restart something. macOS has no setting to pin the keys to one app.

Without Beamhook
Whatever played last wins. The keys wander between Safari, Music, and whatever grabbed focus.
With Beamhook
The one app you picked answers every press. Nothing else gets a look in.

What you get

Small app. One job. Done properly.

No accounts, no background services, no smart-switching to second-guess you. Beamhook lives in the menu bar and does exactly what you tell it.

You pick the target

Choose the single app the keys control, right from the menu bar. It never changes on its own — no guessing, no smart-switching, no surprises.

Nothing else can steal them

Beamhook intercepts the key press at the system level and swallows it, so Safari, YouTube and Apple Music never even see it.

Volume for what's playing

A live list of the apps currently making sound, each with its own volume slider for the ones that are scriptable.

Add any app you like

Spotify, Apple Music, VLC and VOX are built in. Add your own with one line of AppleScript — no rebuild required.

How it works

Three steps, then forget it's there.

01

Grant Accessibility

A one-time macOS permission so Beamhook can capture the hardware media keys. Granted once, it sticks.

02

Pick your app

Open the menu-bar popover and choose your target — Spotify, Music, or anything you've added.

03

Press play

The key goes exactly where you pointed it. That's the whole app — it just gets out of your way.

Works with

Built-in apps, plus anything scriptable.

Spotify Apple Music VLC VOX + any AppleScriptable app

Get started

Build it in about a minute.

Beamhook builds from source with XcodeGen. Clone it, run one script, and the menu-bar icon appears.

# needs Xcode + `brew install xcodegen`
git clone https://github.com/ppixu/beamhook
cd beamhook
./run.sh

run.sh regenerates the Xcode project, signs the build with your Apple Development identity so the Accessibility grant survives rebuilds, compiles, and launches the app.
